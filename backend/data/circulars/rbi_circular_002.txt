RBI Circular No. RBI/2026/002
Date: June 2026
Subject: KYC Re-verification for Dormant Accounts

All banks must complete KYC re-verification for accounts dormant for 
more than 12 months within 60 days.

Banks must ensure:
1. Customer contact within 15 days via registered mobile/email
2. Physical KYC documents collected within 30 days
3. Accounts not re-verified to be frozen after 60 days
4. Audit trail maintained for all KYC activities for 5 years
5. Monthly report to be submitted to RBI on compliance status

Implementation deadline: 60 days from date of circular.

Signed,
Governor, Reserve Bank of India
