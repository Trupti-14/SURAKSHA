RBI Circular No. RBI/2026/001
Date: June 2026
Subject: Mandatory Two-Factor Authentication for Internet Banking

All scheduled commercial banks are hereby directed to implement mandatory 
two-factor authentication (2FA) for all internet banking transactions 
exceeding Rs 10,000. 

Banks must ensure:
1. OTP-based verification for all high-value transactions
2. Biometric authentication option for mobile banking users
3. Session timeout of 5 minutes for inactive sessions
4. Real-time SMS/email alerts for all transactions above Rs 5,000

Implementation deadline: 30 days from date of circular.
Non-compliance will attract penalty under Section 47 of Banking Regulation Act.

Signed,
Governor, Reserve Bank of India
